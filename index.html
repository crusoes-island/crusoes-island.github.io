    Robinson Crusoe

    Eight-and-twenty years upon an island

    You are cast ashore alone. Salvage what you can from the wreck, then hunt, build, sow and tame your way from a frightened castaway to lord of your own island, just as Defoe told it. Passages from the novel appear as you live through each moment of the story.
